Connect your Apple Music account

One time only. The tool needs your account token to create playlists in your library.

  1. Open music.apple.com in this browser and sign in.
  2. Press F12 to open the developer tools.
    Chrome, Edge, Brave: Application tab → Cookies → https://music.apple.com
    Firefox: Storage tab → Cookies → https://music.apple.com
  3. Find media-user-token, copy its whole value and paste it here:

The token stays on this computer (saved in the .env file next to the app) and is only sent to Apple. Don't share it: it gives access to your Apple Music library.
